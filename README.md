
# Healthcare Efficiency Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project provides a deep dive into **151 hospitals across New York** performing hip replacement surgeries.

Using **Power BI, DAX measures, and interactive dashboards**, I analyzed **26,000+ hospital discharges** to uncover the key factors driving:

* Cost per discharge
* Length of Stay (LOS)
* Severity and mortality risk impact
* Regional hospital differences

The goal was to transform complex healthcare data into clear, actionable insights that support operational efficiency and improved patient care.

---

## 📌 Dataset Summary

* **151 hospitals** across New York State
* **26K+ patient discharges analyzed**
* **Average Length of Stay (LOS):** 2.65 days
* **Average Cost per Discharge:** ~$21,000

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

*  **Cost Variability:** Significant cost variation exists across hospitals and regions.
*  **Severity Impact:** High severity cases and extreme mortality risk strongly correlate with increased LOS and higher costs.
*  **Regional Differences:**

  * NYC hospitals show higher average costs and longer LOS compared to upstate facilities.
*  **Discharge Disposition Matters:**

  * Patients discharged to skilled nursing facilities or those who expired had the highest average LOS.
